WITHROOM — ghost voice preview

Every ghost line, its clip, and how it sounds once the game has finished with it.

Files go in assets/audio/voice/ghost/en/<id>.wav (or .mp3). A green dot means the file loaded. Generate them dry and identical — no reverb, no pitch shift, no effects — because play here is the same chain the game uses, and doubling up on processing is what makes a set sound inconsistent. raw is the untouched file, for comparison.

Tone

Applied at play time, so these change how every clip sounds without touching a file. Dial it in here, then paste the numbers into GHOST_VOICE in js/audio/manager.js to make them the game's defaults.

Changes apply to the next line played.
